About me

Hi, I'm Umar. I design product experiences that are clean, intentional, and built to ship. With a computer science background and 4+ years in product design, I sit comfortably at the intersection of design and technology and right now, that means using AI to close the gap between Figma and working software.

've worked across SaaS, mobile, fintech, and enterprise and I'm based in Pakistan, working with teams globally. When I'm not designing, I'm on a cricket pitch 🏏, hiking somewhere new 🥾, or deep in a CSGO match 🎮.

My approach

01

Understand before designing

I spend more time in the problem than most designers are comfortable with. The right interface only makes sense once you understand what's actually being build and for who.

02

Design systems, not screens

Design systems, not screens

A good screen is easy. A good system scales. I design with components, patterns, and logic — so the product stays consistent whether it's 5 screens or 50.

03

Ship, don't just spec

Ship, don't just spec

I use AI tools like Claude Code to take designs beyond Figma. Fewer handoff gaps, faster iteration, and products that actually reach users not just files that sit in a shared drive.
