Overview

Construction project managers run 20+ active job sites at once, each with its own budget, schedule, crew, and risk profile. Portfolio dashboards are supposed to make that manageable. Instead, most force PMs to cross-check every number against another screen before trusting it.

The Problem

Construction is one of the largest industries in the world — and one of the least digitized. Most mid-size firms still coordinate across disconnected tools, leading to delays, budget overruns, and accountability gaps that cost real money.

Without operational automation, campaign execution becomes slower, more expensive, and difficult to scale.

Fragmented communication

Site updates, task assignments, project updates and escalations .Nothing is searchable

Budget visibility

Budget tracking lives in Excel files updated weekly sometimes monthly. By the time its too late.

Unscoped metrics

"Open RFIs: 18" doesn't say if that's one job site or the whole portfolio. A PM can't act on a number without knowing it

Contradidting data

Growing campaign complexity increased operational overhead across teams.

Solution

Structura was rebuilt around one operating principle: every number states its scope, and any two numbers are only allowed to differ if the interface explains why. Getting there wasn't a single redesign pass, it was four distinct stages, each one testing the last.

Structura was rebuilt around one operating principle: every number states its scope, and any two numbers are only allowed to differ if the interface explains why. Getting there wasn’t a single redesign pass, it was four distinct stages, each one testing the last.

Each stage looked at the whole product, not one screen in isolation, so a fix made on Tasks would hold up once it met Calendar and Budget & Cost.
